# Public inputs, attribution and changes

- **MMLU** — Dan Hendrycks et al., [Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03300), [HF release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu/tree/c30699e8356da336a370243923dbaf21066bb9fe/high_school_statistics), [MIT notice](LICENSE-MMLU). Only the high-school statistics subject. IDs use split and zero-based release row; question text and option order retained.
- **MMLU-Redux** — Gema et al., [Are We Done with MMLU?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04127), [annotated data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/edinburgh-dawg/mmlu-redux/tree/3720db6aeb3d019de48bf37916c1a54074ff4997/high_school_statistics). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Retained stems, options, review status, reviewer-suggested choice indices and source row numbers; fixed reversible Latin-1/UTF-8 mojibake and normalized whitespace before joining. “OK” means the reviewer did not flag an issue; it is not an independent blind answer or a guarantee.
- **Hugging Face Open LLM Leaderboard (archive)** — April 19, 2024 five-shot [Llama 3 8B base results](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-llm-leaderboard-old/details_meta-llama__Meta-Llama-3-8B/blob/db0a9a321c769ebd745b28f3509a6f14ee9a3eb5/results_2024-04-19T01-26-07.544774.json) and [Instruct results](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-llm-leaderboard-old/details_meta-llama__Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct/blob/0bb67ddda727931a9e7428fdab9f3654a737b1a8/results_2024-04-19T09-19-13.454877.json). Chose the earliest April 19 runs. Stored only factual argmax choice indices, published accuracies and source URLs; no model, prompts, logits or generated prose redistributed. Matched every answer on full question and ordered options and checked every reconstructed correctness bit against upstream `metrics.acc`.
- **Lookalikes** — [Openbenchmarks Labs](https://openbenchmarks.com/lookalikes), [public API](https://openbenchmarks.com/api/benchmarks/lookalikes), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted published October 16, 2026 P@10 seed-cell counts, judged/missing status and published provider scores to a compact snapshot. No vendor calls, candidate descriptions or judge prompts reproduced. Attribution does not imply endorsement.

The source URLs, pinned revisions and byte digests are in [freeze.py](../scripts/freeze.py). The project MIT license applies to original code, not to upstream data. Reports derived from CC BY inputs retain this attribution.
